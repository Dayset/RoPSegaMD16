Rings of Power — trading guide (ringsofpower.co.uk) vs ROM price table
Guide prices are player-observed; the ROM code wins on disagreement.
ROM base = scaled value of the good's price record (mod 0, no merchant).
$2EA92 buy price = base adjusted by merchant attitude (att 0-100 ->
  x0.64..x1.36), x2 if m20==32 and location==367, /2 if item==14 and
  $1B298[m20*2]==modifier. Sell price = $2EA92(14, slot)/2, so a guide
  sell price S implies a $2EA92 value of 2*S; that must lie in the
  $2EA92 bands [0.64,1.36]xbase (no multiplier) or [1.28,2.72]xbase (x2).
Buy guide prices fit (base/2)*attitude for 17/21 goods (halving active);
the 3 Mesa stones fit base*attitude (no halving); Nectar fits 2*base*
attitude (doubling active at Commerce). Sell guide prices fit the x2
band (doubling active, then /2) for 17/18 goods; Gems 428 is IMPOSSIBLE
(implies $2EA92 = 856 = 4.28x base; max with doubling is 2.72x) — the
guide's Gems sell price is wrong, or Gems sells through a different path.
