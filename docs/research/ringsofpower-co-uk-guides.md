## 1. Sextant (sextant.htm)

- Finding the sextant is one of the most-asked questions on the site.
- Clue 1: merchant **Rashid** (met during the Knight-symbol quest)
  says a pirate hid a magic sextant in a cave behind a waterfall just
  west of Cathedral.
- Clue 2: the receptionist at the Archers Headquarters in **Speed** —
  give her flowers bought in Perfection and she opens the door and
  hints the sextant is in a cave behind the falls just to the east.
- Route: follow the path east out of town; as it turns north, leave
  the path and keep heading east (a shortcut to Cathedral and
  Perfection). A bridge marks the cave location.
- Once owned, the sextant shows map coordinates — essential for
  following directions and for locations.htm.

## 2. Stones (stones.htm)

Five stone types:

- **Bridge stone** — places a bridge over a square of water.
- **Boat stone** — summons a boat (only next to water).
- **Dragon stone** — summons a dragon (only on flat land).
- **Gate stone** — teleports between gates.
- **Flip stone** — teleports to a gate.

Eight gates (coordinates as printed on the page; the world is spelled
"Usha Bau" on the guide pages, "Uska Bau" on the section indexes):

- 0°1″-10°6″ — desert just south of Speed
- 7°4″-6°9″ — just north of the road between Cathedral and Kaos
- 10°8″-2°5″ — island just east of Protection
- 10°9″-15°3″ — just west of Doggonia
- 13°9″-20°9″ — northern tip of the snowbank, just east of Sparta
- 23°6″-6°3″ — peninsula south-west of Oxbridge
- 27°2″-12°7″ — between Celestial City and the Theatre
- 34°4″-16°0″ — island north-west of the Fount of Heaven

## 4. Maze (maze.htm)

- The maze from the **ring of will quest** — "possibly the hardest
  part of the whole game".
- The solution is conveyed by a MAP IMAGE (trees green, stumps brown,
  switches orange, leaf pink; start top-left; "mounds" to activate to
  progress). The images did not load in the archived view, so no
  step-by-step directions could be read as text. The image files live
  in the archive — a junior agent can fetch them (#033).
