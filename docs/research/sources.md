Re-check 2026-11-09 (continuation session): **no decisive new
source; the tension stands and no winner is picked.** One
hedged analytical note: the specific 1991 dates on offer are
January 1 and December 31 — the two conventional placeholder
dates game databases use when only a year (or nothing) is
known — which weakens them as evidence of an actual 1991
street date; Sega-16 likewise gives only the year 1991.
Against that stand the ROM header (1992.JAN) and current
Wikipedia (NA January 1992, EU February 1992, showcased at
the 1991 Summer CES).

Pattern: older/secondary sources say 1991; the ROM header and
current Wikipedia (citing contemporary magazines) say Jan/Feb
1992. A plausible reconciliation is a late-1991 completion /
1992 street date, but **no source in hand states that — do not
pick a winner** (the issue forbids it without evidence). The
magazine citations (GamePro Feb 1992 review exists) make a 1991
*street* date hard to sustain; recorded as tension, not verdict.
